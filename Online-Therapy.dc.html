What it is

What is online therapy?

Online therapy (telehealth) is counseling delivered over a secure, HIPAA-compliant video portal. You talk with the same licensed clinician each session, just as you would in our office.

It’s a good fit if you live outside Albuquerque, have mobility or health concerns, work unpredictable hours, or simply can’t get away. All you need is an internet connection and a private space.
