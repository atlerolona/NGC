What it is

What is individual therapy?

Individual therapy is a private, regular conversation with a licensed clinician focused on you — your goals, your history and what’s getting in the way. Together you build a clear treatment plan and measure progress against it.

Because every person is different, our therapists draw on several proven approaches: Cognitive Behavioral Therapy, Dialectical Behavior Therapy, EMDR, mindfulness, motivational interviewing and psychoanalytic therapy.
