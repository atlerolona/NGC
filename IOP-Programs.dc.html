What it is

What is an intensive outpatient program (IOP)?

An Intensive Outpatient Program is structured addiction treatment you attend several days a week while living at home. You get the accountability and peer support of a group, plus a weekly one-on-one session with your own therapist.

Our IOP follows the Matrix Model — an evidence-based approach that combines relapse prevention, family education, cognitive behavioral therapy and ongoing drug and alcohol testing. Detox is not required to join, but the program works best as the next step after inpatient detox.
