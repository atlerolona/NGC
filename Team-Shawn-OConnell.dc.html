Licensed Professional Clinical Counselor

Shawn O’Connell, LPCC

Therapy is a practical, effective tool for measurable personal growth.

Shawn is a Licensed Professional Clinical Counselor with a strong background in both mental health and public service. He earned his Master’s degree in Education from Wayland Baptist University in 2012, followed by a Master’s degree in Counseling in 2014.

A retired first responder with more than 25 years of supervisory experience, Shawn is widely recognized for his contributions to crisis response in New Mexico. He is credited as one of the first officers to introduce Crisis Intervention Team (CIT) training to the state.

With more than 12 years of experience in community healthcare clinics, Shawn is deeply committed to supporting people with diverse and complex needs. His approach emphasizes resilience and solution-focused strategies, drawing from person-centered modalities.

Shawn is dedicated to creating a supportive environment where clients feel empowered to pursue meaningful change in their lives.
