About New Growth Counseling, a veteran-owned mental health agency in Albuquerque

Our mental health professionals work as a team to identify each client’s goals and build a treatment plan for their path to wellness. We want every client to feel heard, respected and empowered.

Veteran-owned

Why a veteran-owned practice matters for veterans & first responders

Service changes people. As a veteran-owned agency, we built New Growth around people who carry the weight of service, and around their families. Our clinicians include a retired U.S. military colonel and retired first responders, so they understand the culture, the language and the reluctance to ask for help.

Our approach

Evidence-based therapy approaches we use

Every person is unique, so we don’t use one method for everyone. Your clinician picks from several proven approaches and fits them to your goals. If you’re struggling with addiction, we offer both individual and group therapy, including our intensive outpatient program.
