What it is

What is first responder group therapy?

High-stress calls, life-and-death decisions and years of cumulative trauma take a toll. Sometimes that stress turns into coping habits — drinking, substances, other behaviors — that start to cost more than they give.

This outpatient group gives first responders a secure, judgment-free place to address substance use and addictive behaviors with peers who share their experience. It combines CBT, EMDR and peer support, and it’s built to fit around shift work — no inpatient stay required.
